Disciplina Laboratorio de CAD
Professor : Sergio 
Duração : 45h 
Ano : 2022/2023


In this unit, I strengthened my visual culture and developed a solid understanding of graphic and audiovisual communication. I learned to use graphic language effectively, working with the key elements of design—typography, color, image, composition, and layout—to create coherent visual messages. I also developed my ability to analyze and critique graphic and digital communication pieces with a more informed and critical perspective.

Throughout the course, I worked with professional software to edit images, create digital drawings, and develop layouts for web interfaces. I explored bitmap manipulation, color models, retouching techniques, and advanced tools such as paths, filters, and alpha channels. I also learned to build functional web layouts, experimenting with systems of typography, interactivity, mobile-friendly design, and page structure.

By combining technical skills with conceptual thinking, I created digital media projects that communicated ideas clearly and visually. This experience allowed me to connect graphic design practice with real-world applications, improving both my creative expression and my understanding of communication design processes.
